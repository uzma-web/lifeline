How the matching works

  1. 01
    Donors register with blood group, city, and last donation date.

    Someone posts a request with patient, hospital, and blood group needed.
  3. 03
    LifeLine filters to donors who are medically compatible, nearby, and past their 90-day cooldown.
  4. 04
    The requester notifies matched donors directly — no phone numbers exposed until then.
